# Fact Sheet FS01: Reddy v Srinivasulu

Matter: Suit for specific performance of an agreement to sell a house plot
Acting for: Defendant
Jurisdiction: India
Practice area: Property and land

## Parties
- Plaintiff: Venkata Reddy, a businessman in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh.
- Defendant: K. Srinivasulu, owner of a 240 square yard house plot in Tirupati.

## Facts
1. On 4 March 2019 the defendant signed an agreement to sell the plot to the plaintiff for Rs 36,00,000. The plaintiff paid Rs 4,00,000 as earnest money.
2. The agreement required the plaintiff to pay the balance of Rs 32,00,000 and take a registered sale deed within six months, by 3 September 2019, and said time was of the essence.
3. The plaintiff did not pay the balance by 3 September 2019. The defendant sent a legal notice on 20 September 2019 asking him to pay within 15 days, failing which the agreement would stand cancelled and the earnest money forfeited.
4. The plaintiff replied that he was arranging a bank loan, but produced no sanction letter. His bank statements for 2019 show balances below Rs 2,00,000.
5. In January 2022 the plaintiff filed a suit for specific performance. The plaint says in one line that he "was and is ready and willing" to perform, but he did not deposit the balance in court.
6. The defendant says the plaintiff never had the money, that the suit is an attempt to profit from the rise in land prices, and that the agreement was cancelled.

## Client's objective
The defendant wants the suit dismissed on the ground that the plaintiff was never ready and willing to perform, and to keep the earnest money.
