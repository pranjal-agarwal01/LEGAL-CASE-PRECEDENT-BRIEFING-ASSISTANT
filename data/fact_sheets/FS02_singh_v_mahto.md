# Fact Sheet FS02: Singh v Mahto

Matter: Suit for possession of agricultural land against a long occupant claiming adverse possession
Acting for: Plaintiff
Jurisdiction: India
Practice area: Property and land

## Parties
- Plaintiff: Harbans Singh, recorded owner of 3 kanals 10 marlas of agricultural land in village Kheri, district Karnal, Haryana.
- Defendant: Ramesh Mahto, who has cultivated the land since about 2004.

## Facts
1. The plaintiff inherited the land from his father in 1998 under a registered will. The jamabandi for 2001-02 records him as owner.
2. In 2004 the plaintiff moved to Canada. He says he allowed the defendant, a neighbour, to cultivate the land on his behalf and to keep part of the crop.
3. In 2009 the patwari recorded the defendant as being in cultivating possession in the khasra girdawari. In 2016 a mutation was entered in the defendant's name on his application, without notice to the plaintiff.
4. The plaintiff returned in 2021 and asked for the land back. The defendant refused and says he has been in open, continuous and hostile possession for more than twelve years and is now the owner.
5. The defendant has no sale deed or other document of title. He relies on the revenue entries and on his long cultivation.
6. The plaintiff filed a suit for possession in 2022.

## Client's objective
The plaintiff wants possession of the land. He says the defendant's possession was permissive and that revenue entries do not make the defendant the owner.
