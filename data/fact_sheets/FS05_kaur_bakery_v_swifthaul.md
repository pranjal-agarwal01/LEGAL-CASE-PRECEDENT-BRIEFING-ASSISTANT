# Fact Sheet FS05: Kaur Artisan Bakery Ltd v SwiftHaul Couriers Ltd

Matter: Lost profits caused by a late delivery
Acting for: Claimant
Jurisdiction: England and Wales

## Parties
- Claimant: Kaur Artisan Bakery Ltd, a bakery in Coventry.
- Defendant: SwiftHaul Couriers Ltd, a parcel delivery company.

## Facts
1. On 3 September 2025 the controller board of the bakery's only industrial oven failed. The manufacturer agreed to repair it if it was delivered to its workshop in Birmingham.
2. On 4 September 2025 the bakery booked SwiftHaul's guaranteed next-day delivery service online for £45, describing the parcel only as "electronic component". It did not tell SwiftHaul that the bakery could not trade without it.
3. When booking, the bakery's manager ticked a box confirming acceptance of SwiftHaul's terms and conditions. Clause 9 excludes liability "for any loss of profit, business or contracts, however caused, arising from delay".
4. Through SwiftHaul's error the parcel was sent to the wrong depot and was delivered on 15 September 2025, eleven days late.
5. The bakery could not bake for twelve days. It lost ordinary trade profits of about £18,000, and lost a one-off contract worth £40,000 to supply cakes for a celebrity wedding, which SwiftHaul knew nothing about.
6. SwiftHaul says it could not have contemplated that the bakery would be unable to trade, that the wedding contract was exceptional, and that in any event clause 9 excludes all of these losses.

## Client's objective
The bakery wants to claim damages for breach of the delivery contract, including its lost profits and the lost wedding contract.
