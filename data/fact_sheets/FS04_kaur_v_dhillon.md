# Fact Sheet FS04: Kaur v Dhillon

Matter: Sale of land by the defendant while a suit for specific performance was pending
Acting for: Plaintiff
Jurisdiction: India
Practice area: Property and land

## Parties
- Plaintiff: Gurpreet Kaur, purchaser under an agreement to sell.
- Defendant 1: Jaswinder Dhillon, owner of 8 kanals of land in village Sahnewal, district Ludhiana, Punjab.
- Defendant 2: Amarjit Gill, who bought the same land from Defendant 1 in 2021.

## Facts
1. On 15 June 2017 Defendant 1 agreed to sell the land to the plaintiff for Rs 48,00,000 and received Rs 12,00,000 as advance.
2. Defendant 1 later refused to execute the sale deed. The plaintiff filed a suit for specific performance in the Civil Court, Ludhiana, on 2 April 2018 and has attended every hearing.
3. On 9 August 2021, while the suit was pending, Defendant 1 executed a registered sale deed of the same land in favour of Defendant 2 for Rs 70,00,000.
4. Defendant 2 says he is a bona fide purchaser for value without notice of the agreement or of the suit. He lives in the next village, and the suit is noted in the court's cause list that is displayed at the tehsil.
5. Defendant 2 has applied to be added as a party and to have the suit dismissed against the land.

## Client's objective
The plaintiff wants a decree for specific performance binding on Defendant 2, so that the sale deed in his favour does not defeat her claim.
