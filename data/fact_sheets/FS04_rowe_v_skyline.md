# Fact Sheet FS04: Rowe v Skyline Terraces Ltd

Matter: Hire of a rooftop terrace for an event that was cancelled
Acting for: Claimant
Jurisdiction: England and Wales

## Parties
- Claimant: Thomas Rowe, an events organiser.
- Defendant: Skyline Terraces Ltd, owner of a rooftop terrace bar beside the River Thames.

## Facts
1. On 10 May 2025 Mr Rowe agreed to hire Skyline's rooftop terrace for the evening of 5 July 2025 for £12,000 and paid a £4,000 deposit.
2. Skyline's online listing advertised the terrace as "the best view in London of the Thames Victory Fireworks", a display planned to celebrate the city hosting a sporting final. Mr Rowe's emails to Skyline said his guests were coming "to watch the fireworks", and Skyline's reply promised "front-row seats for the display".
3. The written hire agreement described only "exclusive use of the terrace, 6pm to midnight, with bar service" and did not mention the fireworks.
4. On 28 June 2025 the council cancelled the fireworks display after a safety review.
5. The terrace, bar and staff remained available on 5 July 2025. On 29 June 2025 Mr Rowe cancelled his event and asked for his deposit back.
6. Skyline refuses to return the deposit and claims the £8,000 balance. It says the terrace could still be used, that the fireworks were only Mr Rowe's own commercial purpose, and that the event simply became less attractive.

## Client's objective
Mr Rowe argues that the hire contract was frustrated by the cancellation of the fireworks. He wants his deposit back and to avoid paying the balance.
