# Fact Sheet FS06: Adeyemi v Harbourside Hotel Ltd

Matter: Theft from a hotel room and a notice excluding liability
Acting for: Claimant
Jurisdiction: England and Wales

## Parties
- Claimant: Folake Adeyemi, a management consultant.
- Defendant: Harbourside Hotel Ltd, which runs the Harbourside Hotel in Brighton.

## Facts
1. On 12 August 2025 Ms Adeyemi arrived at the Harbourside Hotel without a booking and asked for a room at the reception desk.
2. At reception she signed a registration card recording her name, address and car registration. The card contained no terms and conditions and did not refer to any. She paid for two nights and was given a key card.
3. A notice on the back of her bedroom door stated: "The hotel accepts no liability for loss of or damage to guests' property left in rooms. Valuables must be deposited at reception."
4. The same terms appear on the hotel's website, which Ms Adeyemi had never visited.
5. On 13 August 2025, while she was at dinner, her laptop and a diamond bracelet worth £6,500 in total were stolen from her room. The police found no forced entry; a member of staff had left a master key card unattended on a cleaning trolley.
6. The hotel relies on the bedroom notice to exclude liability and says Ms Adeyemi should have deposited her valuables at reception.

## Client's objective
Ms Adeyemi wants to recover the value of her stolen property and argues that the notice excluding liability is not part of her contract with the hotel.
