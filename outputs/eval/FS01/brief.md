## Key facts

- On 14 March 2025 Arjun Mehta bought two bottles of Fizzleaf kombucha at the Green Cup cafe in Leeds and gave one of them to Ms Nair. — Fact sheet ¶1
- The bottle was dark brown glass and was still sealed with Brightwater's crimped metal cap when it was opened at the table, so the contents could not be seen or inspected. — Fact sheet ¶2
- Ms Nair drank about half of the bottle before noticing a sharp sliver of glass and a mass of mould at the bottom. — Fact sheet ¶3
- That evening she suffered severe abdominal pain and on 15 March 2025 she was admitted to Aireside General Hospital, where a laceration of the oesophagus and stomach lining was found. — Fact sheet ¶4
- Ms Nair has a pre-existing gastric ulcer condition. Her consultant says that the condition made the internal injury far more serious than it would have been for a healthy person. — Fact sheet ¶5
- She was unable to work until 30 June 2025 and needed two further procedures. — Fact sheet ¶6
- Brightwater says its bottling line passed a safety inspection on 1 March 2025 and suggests the cafe may have tampered with the bottle. Its medical expert says that the ulcer, not the glass, caused most of the damage and that she would have needed treatment anyway. — Fact sheet ¶7
