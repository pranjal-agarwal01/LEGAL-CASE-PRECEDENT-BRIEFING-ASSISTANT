## Key facts

- On 12 August 2025 Ms Adeyemi arrived at the Harbourside Hotel without a booking and asked for a room at the reception desk. — Fact sheet ¶1
- At reception she signed a registration card recording her name, address and car registration. The card contained no terms and conditions and did not refer to any. She paid for two nights and was given a key card. — Fact sheet ¶2
- A notice on the back of her bedroom door stated: "The hotel accepts no liability for loss of or damage to guests' property left in rooms. Valuables must be deposited at reception." — Fact sheet ¶3
- The same terms appear on the hotel's website, which Ms Adeyemi had never visited. — Fact sheet ¶4
- On 13 August 2025, while she was at dinner, her laptop and a diamond bracelet worth £6,500 in total were stolen from her room. The police found no forced entry; a member of staff had left a master key card unattended on a cleaning trolley. — Fact sheet ¶5
- The hotel relies on the bedroom notice to exclude liability and says Ms Adeyemi should have deposited her valuables at reception. — Fact sheet ¶6
